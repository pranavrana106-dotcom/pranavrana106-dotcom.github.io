Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

This privacy policy describes how the private YouTube publishing tool (the "Tool") used to operate the Whispers with Ivy YouTube channel handles data. The Tool is used solely by the channel owner to manage their own YouTube channel.

What the Tool accesses

With the channel owner's explicit authorization through Google OAuth, the Tool accesses the owner's YouTube account only to:

  • upload videos to the owner's channel;
  • set a video's title, description, tags, category, and scheduled publish time;
  • read basic channel and video details needed to perform those actions.

The Tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What the Tool does not do

  • It does not sell, share, or transfer any data to third parties.
  • It does not use the data for advertising.
  • It does not access any account other than the owner's own channel.
  • It does not collect data from visitors to this website.

Data storage

OAuth access tokens are stored only on the channel owner's own computer and are used only to authenticate requests to YouTube. They can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Contact

For any questions about this policy, contact the owner at pranav87rana@gmail.com.
